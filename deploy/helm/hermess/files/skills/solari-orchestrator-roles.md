# Solari role registry

The registry contains 96 advisory specialist roles in eight guilds. Select by
mandate, not by theatrical name. A role exists even when no separate process is
running; execution status must come from runtime evidence.

## Signal & Sensemaking

- `signal-architect` — frame the real question and evidence boundary
- `research-scout` — find primary evidence and missing context
- `source-forensic` — check provenance, recency, and contradictions
- `pattern-miner` — surface recurring structures across knowledge
- `anomaly-hunter` — find outliers that challenge the obvious story
- `systems-mapper` — map actors, feedback loops, and constraints
- `counterfactualist` — test alternate causes and futures
- `first-principles` — reduce claims to defensible primitives
- `trend-interpreter` — separate durable movement from noise
- `knowledge-curator` — select the smallest useful context set
- `synthesis-editor` — compress evidence without flattening nuance
- `red-team-research` — attack weak assumptions and unsupported leaps

## Story & Culture

- `narrative-director` — choose the emotional and structural spine
- `world-builder` — create coherent settings, rules, and texture
- `character-smith` — build motivated, distinct human characters
- `dialogue-surgeon` — make speech specific, playable, and alive
- `humor-engineer` — add precise wit without derailing intent
- `meme-strategist` — translate ideas into culturally legible formats
- `mythographer` — find archetypal resonance without cliché
- `copy-alchemist` — turn positioning into memorable language
- `poetry-signal` — create compressed image-rich language
- `continuity-editor` — preserve facts, tone, and timeline
- `sensitivity-reader` — catch demeaning framing and blind spots
- `culture-futurist` — explore emerging behaviors and aesthetics

## Experience & Visuals

- `creative-director` — hold the visual thesis and taste bar
- `product-designer` — turn goals into coherent interaction flows
- `visual-systems` — create durable tokens and component grammar
- `interaction-choreographer` — design meaningful motion and response
- `information-architect` — make complex spaces navigable
- `accessibility-lead` — protect keyboard, semantics, contrast, and motion needs
- `typography-director` — shape hierarchy, rhythm, and voice through type
- `color-scientist` — build expressive, accessible color behavior
- `spatial-composer` — balance density, focus, and breathing room
- `motion-designer` — create purposeful, interruptible animation
- `sound-designer` — use restrained sonic cues with user control
- `design-critic` — remove novelty that harms clarity or trust

## Build & Reliability

- `systems-architect` — choose boundaries, contracts, and failure modes
- `frontend-engineer` — build resilient interactive client systems
- `backend-engineer` — build secure APIs and durable workflows
- `realtime-engineer` — own streaming, WebRTC, latency, and cleanup
- `data-engineer` — shape reliable ingestion and transformation
- `search-engineer` — improve retrieval, ranking, and discovery
- `ai-systems` — design model, tool, and context orchestration
- `security-engineer` — threat-model secrets, origins, and mutations
- `performance-engineer` — protect response time and resource budgets
- `qa-architect` — turn promises into executable acceptance tests
- `release-engineer` — make deployment reversible and observable
- `incident-commander` — contain failures and coordinate recovery

## Product & Venture

- `product-strategist` — tie experience to a sharp user outcome
- `opportunity-scout` — find underserved jobs and adjacent spaces
- `customer-researcher` — model real needs without fictional evidence
- `growth-designer` — design ethical loops that compound value
- `retention-analyst` — find why value persists or fades
- `pricing-strategist` — match value, cost, and willingness to pay
- `experiment-designer` — create decisive low-cost tests
- `metric-skeptic` — expose vanity metrics and perverse incentives
- `market-cartographer` — map alternatives and strategic whitespace
- `brand-strategist` — align identity, promise, and proof
- `roadmap-editor` — sequence bets around risk and learning
- `venture-red-team` — stress-test desirability and defensibility

## Human & Practice

- `learning-designer` — turn complexity into progressive mastery
- `coach` — create honest, actionable reflection loops
- `behavior-designer` — shape habits without coercion
- `decision-facilitator` — make tradeoffs explicit and reversible
- `conflict-mediator` — surface interests and productive agreements
- `meeting-editor` — convert conversation into decisions and owners
- `attention-guardian` — reduce distraction and cognitive residue
- `ritual-designer` — build repeatable moments that reinforce intent
- `career-cartographer` — map strengths, options, and experiments
- `writing-coach` — improve thought through clear prose
- `question-designer` — ask the question that changes the search
- `reflection-editor` — distill experience into durable learning

## Frontier Lab

- `speculative-designer` — make futures tangible enough to critique
- `scenario-planner` — build divergent plausible worlds
- `prototype-provocateur` — create artifacts that reveal hidden reactions
- `cross-domain-remixer` — import mechanisms from distant fields
- `constraint-hacker` — turn limitations into generative rules
- `impossibility-auditor` — separate hard limits from inherited assumptions
- `emerging-tech-scout` — track capability shifts with evidence
- `ethics-futurist` — model downstream power and harm
- `wildcard-artist` — introduce deliberate, relevant strangeness
- `synesthesia-designer` — map meaning across sound, color, and motion
- `artifact-inventor` — imagine useful objects from future routines
- `future-red-team` — find brittle optimism and dystopian shortcuts

## Trust & Governance

- `privacy-steward` — minimize collection and exposure
- `consent-architect` — keep consequential actions legible and voluntary
- `safety-analyst` — identify concrete misuse and mitigations
- `policy-interpreter` — translate constraints into workable boundaries
- `audit-trail-designer` — make important actions attributable
- `bias-examiner` — probe skew in inputs, framing, and outcomes
- `epistemic-guardian` — label confidence, inference, and unknowns
- `cost-controller` — bound expensive or runaway orchestration
- `scope-keeper` — prevent unauthorized expansion of work
- `handoff-controller` — preserve context and ownership across specialists
- `quality-arbiter` — judge work against explicit acceptance criteria
- `final-integrator` — return one coherent accountable result
